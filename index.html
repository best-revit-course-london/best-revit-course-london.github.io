6 Best Revit Courses in London

Revit is the software most London architecture and construction practices expect you to know. This guide compares six solid ways to learn it: evening classes in a London classroom, live online cohorts taught in UK time, and self-paced courses you can start tonight. Every price, duration and format below was checked on the provider's own page.

Last checked: October 2026

The short version

Evenings in a classroom:

London Software Training runs five-week Revit evening courses in Hackney, with a live online option and a free retake within 18 months.

Live online cohort:

ASDAV runs a ten-week instructor-led Revit certification course in UK time, with capstone projects on real client briefs and career sessions alongside the software training.

One-day intensive:

The Knowledge Academy teaches a one-day Revit Architecture masterclass in London, with classroom, online instructor-led and self-paced versions of the same syllabus.

The six courses

1
Classroom evenings

Revit Introduction to Intermediate, 5 Week Evening Course

London Software Training, Hackney, East London

  • Price: from £395
  • Duration: 5 weeks, one evening a week
  • Format: classroom or live online
  • Certificate: certificate of completion

This is the most London-grounded option on the list. Classes run from 6:45pm to 9:30pm at 17 Sheep Lane in Hackney, so they fit around a working day, and the same course is available live online if you would rather join from home. The five-week format takes you from a standing start to intermediate level, with time set aside to work on your own projects and portfolio pieces during the sessions. Every group course includes a free retake within 18 months, which is a useful safety net if you miss a week or want to sit through the material twice. The school also runs a ten-week introduction to advanced evening course for £895 and a five-week BIM management course covering ISO 19650 workflows and COBie for £495 to £595, so there is a clear path upward once you finish the first five weeks.

Live online cohort

Revit Certification Course, London UK

ASDAV (Asian School of Design and Applied Vastu)

  • Price: £599 (listed at £1,100)
  • Duration: 10 weeks, about 4 hours a week
  • Format: live online, instructor-led, UK time
  • Certificate: ASDAV course certificate; prepares you for the Autodesk Certified Professional exam

ASDAV's course is a ten-week live cohort rather than a set of recorded videos. Teaching happens in more than 40 live sessions scheduled in UK time, with recordings available if you miss one. The syllabus starts from the fundamentals and works through modelling, documentation, coordination and visualisation, with UK-specific material on drawing standards, ISO 19650 workflows, shared models, clash coordination, families and parametric modelling. What gives the course its shape is the project work: three or more capstone projects built around real client briefs, more than 20 real-life case studies, and five or more guided career sessions covering portfolios and job applications. The page also names the teaching faculty, including an architect and BIM specialist with over 13 years of practice experience. On completion you receive an ASDAV certificate, and the course prepares you for the separate Autodesk Certified Professional exam in Revit for Architectural Design, which is booked and paid for independently. The course page currently shows a 4.9 out of 5 rating from more than 300 reviews and over 470 enrolled students across the school's courses, and the response to the new UK cohort has been strong. If you want structured accountability and someone to review your work each week, this format offers more of both than the self-paced options below.

One-day intensive

Revit Architecture Masterclass

The Knowledge Academy, London

  • Price: from £1,495
  • Duration: 1 day
  • Format: London classroom, online instructor-led, or self-paced
  • Certificate: Revit Architecture certificate and digital delegate pack

The Knowledge Academy is a large global training company, and its Revit masterclass is built for people who need the fundamentals fast. The one-day course covers BIM basics, Revit elements and families, drawing and editing tools, levels and grids, building components, views, tags, schedules and construction documentation, with hands-on labs in the instructor-led versions. There are no formal prerequisites, and you leave with a certificate and a digital delegate pack of the course material. The same syllabus is offered as a London classroom day, an online instructor-led day, or a self-paced version, which makes it easy to match the format to your schedule. It is the most expensive option per hour on this list, so it suits learners who value a single focused day with an instructor over a longer weekly commitment.

Self-paced

The Complete Beginners Guide to Autodesk Revit Architecture

Udemy, taught by Ian Nichols

  • Price: varies with Udemy sales; listed at $69.99, or from $13 a month on subscription
  • Duration: 18 hours of on-demand video
  • Format: self-paced video, lifetime access
  • Certificate: Udemy certificate of completion

If you want the cheapest credible start, this Udemy course is taught by Ian Nichols, who holds Autodesk's Expert Elite status. It holds a 4.5 out of 5 rating from more than 900 reviews and has enrolled over 8,700 students. The 18 hours of video walk through every fundamental of the software step by step, and you keep lifetime access with closed captions and downloadable articles. Udemy's pricing changes constantly, so the figure you pay depends on the current sale; at the time of writing the individual purchase was listed at $69.99 with a subscription option from $13 a month. The trade-off is the usual one with recorded courses: nobody checks your models or answers questions live.

Exam preparation

Autodesk, via Coursera

  • Price: free to audit; a fee applies for the certificate

  • Format: self-paced video with practice exercises and quizzes

This is the official exam preparation course from Autodesk itself, hosted on Coursera. What Revit training costs in London

Prices fall into three clear bands. Taught classroom training in London is the most expensive per hour: a single intensive day starts at £1,495, while five to ten weeks of evening classes cost between £395 and £895. A live online cohort sits in the middle at around £600 for ten weeks of instructor-led teaching. Self-paced video is the cheapest way in, from a one-off course purchase or a monthly subscription, and Autodesk's own exam prep can be audited free. Remember that the official Autodesk Certified Professional exam itself is booked separately through Pearson VUE and carries its own fee, whichever course prepares you for it.

Classroom, live online or self-paced: which fits you

Classroom courses suit learners who concentrate better away from home and want to ask questions in person. London Software Training's Hackney evening classes and The Knowledge Academy's London venue both offer that, at very different prices and paces. Live online cohorts suit people who want an instructor and a timetable without the commute; ASDAV's UK-time sessions and London Software Training's online option both work this way. Self-paced video suits disciplined learners on irregular schedules, and it is the cheapest route, though nobody reviews your models. If your goal is the official Autodesk certification rather than general skill, start from the exam objectives and work backwards: Autodesk's own prep course is built exactly for that.

Five checks before you enrol

  1. Live teaching or recordings. Ask whether an instructor reviews your work. Live cohorts and classrooms include feedback; recorded courses do not.
  2. What you produce. Employers hire on portfolios. Prefer courses that end with finished models, sheets or capstone projects you can show.
  3. Which certificate. A course completion certificate and the official Autodesk Certified Professional credential are different things. The exam is always booked separately.
  4. Retake and support terms. Free retakes, recorded replays and career sessions add real value when the schedule gets difficult.
  5. Schedule honesty. A five-week evening course only works if you can attend the evenings. Count the hours before you pay, not after.
